 Kids meet Jazz! with May J. Special Concert" will feature May J. on trumpet, Kunikazu Tanaka on saxophone, Marie Takeda on piano, Kento Yokota on bass, and Ryoma Tada on drums. Under the theme of "Hajimete no Jazz (First Time Jazz)," which can be enjoyed by both parents and children, the program will include familiar favorites such as "Let It Go" and "Beauty and the Beast" from Disney, the shoka "Do Remi no Uta," and "Senroku wa nazuno yo dokodemo doko e" (The Train Track Continues to Go, to Everywhere). We hope you will enjoy a time of exciting rhythms and moving voices in the spacious environment of Kitatopia Sakura Hall.
